How it works

Every contribution has a history.

01 · CORTEX UNITS

Provide the data

Publish source texts and token Frags. A Blueprint pins the corpus, tokenizer and partitions.

Source links · content hashes · deduplicated pulls
02 · TRAINING NODES

Contribute the work

An Execution Lease binds a shard to its parent checkpoint. Trainers compute updates and publish signed contributions.

Forward pass · backpropagation · optimizer steps
03 · DAEMONS

Verify the result

Reconstruct inputs, replay training contributions and calculate the next checkpoint from accepted updates.

Token replay · vector reconstruction · weight checks
04 · THE ORACLE

Certify the history

Validators agree on the ledger. Required verification receipts promote contributions into certified checkpoints.

CometBFT · signed receipts · weight lineage

Discover by role

The Collective uses signed, expiring DHT records to discover peers by role and model profile. Blueprints and training manifests define the work each contribution belongs to.

Rebuild from commitments

Content hashes identify the exact source, tokens and model artifacts. A verifier can retrieve the inputs, repeat the recipe and compare the resulting checkpoint.
